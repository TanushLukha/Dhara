Listed: sections in the Act's arrangement of sections. Found: sections cut from the body, in the arrangement's order. Gaps in the numbering: whole numbers up to the last, and single letters up to the last for a number (171A to 171I), that the arrangement does not list. Unused lines: lines in the central text that look like a section start but were not used as one (a cross reference that breaks over a line, so that its section number starts the line). Headings that differ: the body's heading against the arrangement's, after case and punctuation; the body's is the one used. Lettered: sections such as 54A, and 171I, which India Code prints with a hyphen before the letter.

Every listed section was found in the body, and no number is missing from the arrangements.

Footnotes: the amendment history below the rule at the foot of a page, kept with the section whose marker they carry. State amendment blocks: the state amendments India Code prints after a section, cut out of it and kept beside it, since Dhara answers on the central Acts (decision 2); state insertions are the section like lines inside them. Groups: the headings of groups of sections within a chapter ("Of theft", "ADMISSIONS"). The arrangement names the group of each section; the body's group headings are cut from the end of the section before them, and the two are compared.
